Legal & Privacy

User Data Deletion

We believe your data belongs to you. Here's exactly how we handle it — and how you can request deletion at any time.

🗑

Automatic deletion after 7 days

All conversation data is automatically purged from our servers on a 7-day cycle. In most cases, no action is needed on your part.

What data we collect

When you interact with a Buune-powered WhatsApp assistant, we temporarily hold:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of your support conversation
  • Message timestamps and delivery metadata
  • Any details you shared to resolve your issue (e.g. order numbers)

We do not collect location data, profile information, or anything beyond what is needed to answer your support query.

Request immediate deletion

Want your data removed before the 7-day automatic cycle? Send us an email — we'll confirm deletion within 48 hours.

  1. Email david@grandelevationsolar.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include your WhatsApp phone number with country code (e.g. +234...)
  3. We'll confirm deletion within 48 hours via reply
