Warranty Information

Warranty & Returns Policy

At Grand Elevation Solar, we stand behind the quality of our products. If your product develops a fault during normal use, we'll work with you to repair or replace it in accordance with the policy below.

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

2-Year Warranty

Every eligible Grand Elevation Solar product is covered by a 2-year limited warranty from the original purchase date.

This warranty covers manufacturing defects and failures that occur during normal use.

Warranty Eligibility

To qualify for warranty service, all of the following conditions must be met:

  • The product was purchased less than 2 years ago.
  • The customer can provide proof of purchase.
  • The customer can demonstrate that the product has been used correctly and has not been intentionally damaged, abused, modified, or misused.
  • The fault is not the result of accidental damage, neglect, improper installation, or unauthorized repairs.

Repair & Replacement

If your warranty claim is approved, Grand Elevation Solar will first attempt to repair the product at no cost to you.

If the product cannot reasonably be repaired, we may provide a replacement product of the same model or an equivalent model at our discretion.

Refund Policy

Grand Elevation Solar does not offer refunds for products covered under this warranty.

Our warranty remedy is limited to repair or replacement of eligible products.

How to Make a Warranty Claim

  • Contact our support team.
  • Provide your proof of purchase.
  • Describe the issue and, where requested, provide photos or videos showing the fault.
  • Follow any return or inspection instructions provided by our support staff.
Important: Products that show evidence of intentional damage, misuse, unauthorized modification, or improper handling are not covered by this warranty.
